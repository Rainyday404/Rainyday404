## 『 眠れない夜の哲学 — PHILOSOPHY OF THE SLEEPLESS 』

<br>

<table align="center" width="88%">
<tr>
<td style="padding: 28px; line-height: 2.2;">

<br>

### ◦ 夜と静けさについて &nbsp;·&nbsp; On Night & Silence

> *眠れないことは、弱さじゃない。*
> *ただ、夜が自分を手放してくれないだけだ。*

> *"Not being able to sleep isn't weakness.*
> *The night just isn't done with you yet."*

<br>

### ◦ 存在することについて &nbsp;·&nbsp; On Simply Being

> *誰も見ていない時間に、やっと息ができる気がする。*
> *昼間の自分は、誰かのための自分だから。*

> *"In the hours no one's watching,*
> *I finally feel like I can breathe.*
> *Daytime-me belongs to everyone else."*

<br>

### ◦ 街と孤独について &nbsp;·&nbsp; On The City & Solitude

> *夜の街は、優しい。*
> *昼間みたいに、急かさないから。*

> *"The city at night is gentle.*
> *It doesn't rush you like it does in the day."*

<br>

### ◦ コードと創造について &nbsp;·&nbsp; On Code & Making

> *夜中の3時に書いたコードには、*
> *昼間には出ない、何かが宿っている気がする。*

> *"Code written at 3AM carries something*
> *that daylight hours can't quite replicate.*
> *I don't know what it is. But it's there."*

<br>

### ◦ 朝について &nbsp;·&nbsp; On Morning

> *朝が来るのが、少し惜しい。*
> *でも、また夜が来ることも知っている。*

> *"I'm always a little reluctant when morning comes.*
> *But I know the night will return.*
> *It always does."*

<br>

  I don't know what brought you here.
  Maybe curiosity. Maybe boredom.
  Maybe you also couldn't sleep tonight.

  Either way — hello.
  I hope the glow of your screen
  is keeping you company,
  the way mine is keeping me.

<br>

```
  To the city outside my window —

  Thank you for staying lit.
  For the distant hum of cars
  and the silence between them.
  You make the night feel
  less like an absence
  and more like a room
  I'm just learning to sit in.
```

  To the songs I keep replaying —

  You say things I can't.
  You fill the spaces where
  I run out of words.
